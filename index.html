A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Your questions,
answered.

Understand the commitment before taking the next step.

What is a Noor Circle?+

A small, weekly neighbourhood gathering for 8–10 children aged 6–13, led by a trained mentor. It brings together Qur’an, Tarbiyah, activities and community.

Is there a fee to participate?+

No. Participation is free for parents, mentors and hosts. Families and supporters who are able to can make a voluntary contribution, from PKR 250 a month, to help sustain the circles and reward students and mentors. It is never a condition of joining.

Do mentors need to be scholars?+

The programme welcomes caring, committed applicants and provides mentor training in child psychology, Islamic Tarbiyah, facilitation and curriculum delivery. Discuss suitability and onboarding with the team.

What happens if there is no circle nearby?+

You can express interest in a new circle in your neighbourhood, or help as a mentor or host. Availability and launch timing need to be confirmed with the organisers.

What should parents confirm before enrolment?+

Ask the team about the mentor, venue, age grouping, supervision, safeguarding arrangements, pickup procedure and parent communication before your child attends.

Does this replace school or learning at home?+

Noor Circles is designed to complement schooling and family Tarbiyah through a consistent weekly gathering and everyday practice.
